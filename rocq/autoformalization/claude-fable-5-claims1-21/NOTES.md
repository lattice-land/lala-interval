# Notes on the Rocq formalization

All 21 `\autorocq` claims of the paper are formalized in `claim1.v` … `claim21.v`
(Rocq 9.1.1, `-Q . Paper`). The whole project compiles from scratch with zero
`Admitted`/`Axiom`; every `claimN` theorem depends only on the four standard
classical axioms (`classic`, `constructive_indefinite_description`,
`functional_extensionality_dep`, `propositional_extensionality`).

Remarks on the numbers:

- **Claim 20 dominates**, as expected: it contains the paper's main result
  (best-ness of the four division propagators over unbounded intervals),
  including the α-completeness of the fdiv⁺ core and the structural
  decomposition theorems (union → store join, sign-flip conjugation,
  pre-meet → relation restriction) reused by claims 17, 18 and 21.
- **Claim 17's cost is inflated** by two sub-agent failures unrelated to the
  mathematics (one response exceeded the 64k output-token cap; one run was cut
  by a usage limit and restarted from scratch).
- Claims 1–14 and 19 (the abstract lattice/Galois layer) together took under
  30 minutes and ~250k output tokens.
- The wall-clock span of the session was much longer (Jul 26 12:12 → Jul 27
  09:00, including overnight usage-limit pauses); the *active time* metric
  above excludes those pauses.

## Formalization conventions (summary)

- Quotient lattices (D, Ĩ, **I**) are ordered setoids (`OSet`): an equivalence
  `oeq` with antisymmetry of `ole` up to `oeq`; complete lattices provide
  `csup`, with infima derived. Suprema on ℤ∞ are obtained classically
  (bounded sets of integers have maxima + `epsilon`).
- The best propagator is `bestI c d` / `bestR R d` = interval hull of the
  solutions inside the box γ(d); it is *definitionally* the composite
  α_I ∘ α× ∘ S⟦c⟧ ∘ γ× ∘ γ_I of the paper (`bestI_is_composite_best`).
- Propagators over the three-variable scope use the closed type
  `V3 = Vx | Vy | Vz`; algorithms with the paper's `d(x) ← E` early-return
  updates are encoded by computing the refinement intervals first and
  dispatching on emptiness (`isbotb`), which returns the same store.
- `tdivZ` is defined by the paper's sign criterion (floor of the real
  quotient if it is ≥ 0, ceiling otherwise) and proven equal to Coq's
  `Z.quot` (`tdivZ_quot` in claim1.v).
